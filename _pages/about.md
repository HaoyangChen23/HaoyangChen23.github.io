I'm Haoyang Chen, a Research Assistant at the [UMN APEX Lab](https://caiwending.github.io/), advised by [Prof. Caiwen Ding](https://caiwending.github.io/). I received my B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities in May 2026. I also work closely with [Shiyang Li](https://nku-yang.github.io/) and [Yuebo Luo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yuebo-luo-0513b0241/en).

My research aims to turn World Action Models (WAMs) into robot policies that work in the real world. This requires three coupled properties, which I approach through algorithm-system co-design:

- **Long-horizon consistency:** persistent memory and state, so a WAM stays consistent with what it has already seen and done over long tasks.
- **Efficient inference:** fast enough for closed-loop control on real robots. I contribute to [vLLM-Omni](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-omni) on WAM inference optimization.
- **Robustness:** policies that hold up under the variability of real environments and user demands, beyond curated benchmarks.

I'm applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.
